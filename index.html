Frequently Asked Questions

Why choose DZR?

Here at DZR, we personally believe our services are the very best. From our quick and interactive feedback system to our rapid turnover time, the product we offer is one-of-a-kind, allowing you to come to us for all your social media needs.

What services do you offer for drivers and teams?

We provide full media coverage from graphic design, videography and photography to enhance and boost your social media presence. We also offer social media management to drivers who want to improve the presentation of their social media pages.

What do you need to provide?

As a client you’ll need to send us high quality images of you on and off the track. This gives us a good selection when starting the design process. Additionally we require all information regarding the event such as track, date, round number etc. Sponsor and team logos are also available to be added to the design if necessary.

How long does the process take?

For our graphic design services, we advise giving a 2 weeks notice prior to the finish date, to ensure the graphic is fully prepared and ready to post, allowing for a smooth and stress-free process. For our photography and videography services, get in touch through our email and social media platforms for photo and video editing delivery times.

How much will the service cost?

Our graphic design service starts at Ð200/graphic however we offer bulk order discounts for full season and multiple design packages - in which you can enquire about through our email and social media platforms. For videography and photography please get in touch through our email and social media platforms, and for more information on which events we’ll be attending and what packages we offer.
